Ever heard from other people that they think you come from another country despite being 100% from your country. Can people around the world see the difference between a Norwegian and a Swedish person? Or a Chinese and Korean person? Or a Spanish and a Italian person?

Guess other people's ethnicities, earn points and climb the leaderboard. Find out other people's perception of your ethnicity.
