[Palia Portal](https://palia.app) is a fan-made community website for the cozy MMO [Palia](https://www.palia.com). It serves as a hub encapsulating the companion sites I've built for the game:

- **[Palia Party](https://paliaparty.app)** ([palia.party](https://palia.party)) — find and create parties for in-game activities like cooking, hunting, fishing, home tours and more.
- **[Palia Trading](https://paliatrading.app)** ([palia.trading](https://palia.trading)) — trade plushies, potato pods, stickers and artifacts with other players.

The portal also aggregates Palia news, Twitch streams and a community spotlight.
